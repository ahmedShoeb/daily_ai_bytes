## Key Points

- Paper addresses memory bottlenecks in training Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models at long context lengths or large batch sizes
- Identifies four memory peaks that remain unbounded by common parallelism plans: expert dispatch, vocabulary projection, gradient checkpoint boundaries, and optimizer state
- Proposes four specialized techniques to bound all memory peaks simultaneously: PipelinedLLEP, Ring-DTP, Selective checkpoint offload (SCO), and OffloadStreamAdamW
- Methods maintain exact loss and gradients while only changing order and granularity of computation/data movement
- PipelinedLLEP reduces MoE dispatch peak by up to 59.3% without throughput loss
- Ring-DTP reduces vocabulary projection peak by 86.6%
- OffloadStreamAdamW makes optimizer step 2.05× faster
- Combined techniques enable training MoE models from 120B to 667B parameters at 1M context length
- Achieves 8-32× longer context reach compared to FSDP2 baseline with up to 10.4× throughput improvement
