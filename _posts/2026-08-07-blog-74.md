## Key Points

- Mistral AI has released Shieldstral, a 3B parameter open-weights multimodal safety classifier
- The model takes a unique approach by allowing users to write safety policies as plain-language questions at inference time rather than baking fixed harm categories into weights
- Shieldstral outperforms open guard models up to 7x its size on text safety benchmarks and sets new state-of-the-art on multimodal moderation
- It provides continuous safety scores calibrated to specific contexts, allowing different thresholds for different applications
- The model supports text, image, and text+image content moderation through a single natural-language interface
- Shieldstral is released under Apache 2.0 license as part of Mistral's participation in the Open Secure AI Alliance with NVIDIA
- The model runs on a single 16GB GPU, making it accessible for various deployment scenarios
- Mistral built Shieldstral using their Forge platform, focusing on data quality and heterogeneous training sources to enable policy adaptability
