## Key Points

- Salesforce has announced AIforce, a new 'live interface layer' designed to streamline data access for agentic AI
- AIforce aims to help customers 'break free of a shared user interface' and create personalized, dynamic interfaces for each individual
- Users can build custom interfaces using natural language prompts rather than navigating fixed systems, allowing them to 'compose customized live interfaces on the fly'
- The system will be underpinned by three new services: Claudeforce, Slackforce (for creating applications within Slack), and Agentforce Coworker (an AI teammate within Lightning interface)
- AIforce builds on Salesforce's previous Headless 360 announcement and focuses on providing deeper access to Salesforce's platform data without dedicated browser interfaces
- The goal is to 'unlock trapped value' by giving AI agents deeper access to company records and data sources
- The new approach positions the Salesforce platform as more accessible to users who've been locked out of traditional Salesforce interfaces
- AIforce was unveiled ahead of Salesforce's annual Dreamforce conference
