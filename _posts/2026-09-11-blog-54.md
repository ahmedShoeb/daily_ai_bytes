## Key Points

- Mercury 2.5 is Inception Labs' most capable production model with significant quality improvements over Mercury 2
- It's described as the most capable diffusion LLM on the market and potentially the largest diffusion language model ever trained
- Key performance metrics: 40% increase in intelligence from Mercury 2, 1,107 tokens/second on NVIDIA GPUs, 260K token context window
- Pricing at $0.20 per million input and $0.75 per million output tokens, with 80% launch discount ($0.04/$0.15)
- Used by thousands of developers and dozens of enterprises in production for search, voice, and coding applications
- Mercury Voice and Mercury Router announced as previews alongside Mercury 2.5
- Mercury Voice delivers time-to-first-token under 170ms for voice agents
- Mercury Router intelligently routes prompts to optimal models based on quality, speed, and cost
- Available through Inception API, Baseten, and OpenRouter platforms
- Next model already in training with planned release in coming months
