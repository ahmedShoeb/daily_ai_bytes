## Key Points

- A new U.S. Census Bureau study reveals an unprecedented worsening in outcomes for college graduates in AI-exposed majors since ChatGPT's launch
- Since ChatGPT launched in late 2022, graduates from the most AI-exposed academic majors have seen a 5 percentage point drop in initial employment probability
- These graduates have also experienced a 13% decline in initial quarterly earnings compared to graduates from less AI-exposed majors
- Roughly half of the earnings decline comes from lower pay within industries, while the other half comes from graduates being pushed into lower-wage sectors like retail and restaurants
- The post-ChatGPT earnings shock for the most exposed majors is larger than the normal effect of graduating into a severe recession (9-10% penalty)
- The study represents methodologically strong work despite typical concerns about such research
