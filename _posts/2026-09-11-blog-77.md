## Key Points

- Google DeepMind introduces AlphaGenome Atlas, a database predicting effects of every possible single nucleotide variant in the human genome
- The atlas contains predictions for all 9 billion single-letter genetic changes, resulting in a massive 1-petabyte dataset
- Features AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score that combines predictions for both coding and non-coding DNA regions
- Helps scientists prioritize research without sifting through thousands of data points
- Already accelerating rare disease research - helped Broad Institute solve a case involving DNM1 gene variant
- Applied to UK Biobank data, uncovered 22% more non-coding genetic associations for complex traits like BMI
- Identified 19 genetic regions linked to body mass index (BMI) when focusing on top 1% of impactful variants
- Available through an intuitive website portal requiring zero coding skills, democratizing access for researchers worldwide
- Addresses the 98% of human genome that doesn't code for proteins but remains largely mysterious
- Part of ongoing commitment to accelerate genomic discovery and science for everyone
