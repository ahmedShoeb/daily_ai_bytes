## Key Points

Xcode 27 was released on September 14, 2026 as build 27A266a, alongside iOS 27.0 and other operating system updates
It requires macOS Tahoe 26.6 or later and only runs on Apple silicon Macs (Intel Macs are no longer supported)
Minimum deployment targets were raised: macOS from 11 to 12, watchOS from 8 to 9, and DriverKit from 20 to 21
Three hard breaking changes: the @State macro implementation causes compilation failures in some cases, SE-0508 source break affects computed properties with init accessors, and the -ld64 linker flag fails
The ld64 linker has been completely removed, with -ld_classic now generating warnings
PreviewProvider and related preview modifiers are deprecated in favor of #Preview syntax
Universal Mac app building changes: ARCHS_STANDARD no longer includes x86_64 by default for macOS 27.0 targets
Apple silicon Macs are now mandatory for development - there's no workaround for the build machine requirement
App Store Connect began accepting builds made with Xcode 27 SDKs on September 14, 2026
