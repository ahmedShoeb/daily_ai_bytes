## Key Points

- First unverified benchmark results for Apple's M6 chip surfaced in Geekbench 7 database
- M6 chip scored 4,071 in single-core CPU and 22,783 in multi-core CPU tests
- Represents ~12% faster single-core performance and ~27% faster multi-core performance compared to M5 chip
- M6 chip features 2nm architecture and will debut in the new Mac mini
- Apple claims M6 has 'world's fastest single-threaded CPU performance', with scores topping all other Macs and PCs
- In multi-core performance ranking, M6 chip slots between M1 Ultra (23,328) and M5 (17,948)
- M6 chip is more than 2.5x faster than Apple's original M1 chip
- New Mac mini with M6 and M5 Pro chips launches September 22
- GPU benchmark results for M6 chip are still pending
