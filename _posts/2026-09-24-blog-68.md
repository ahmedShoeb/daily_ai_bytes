## Key Points

- Ungoverned AI is the compounding risk that affects all other risks, embedded in existing licensed software and deployed without proper review processes
- Agentic AI bypasses traditional monitoring tools designed for human users, chaining actions across systems faster than human review cycles can track
- Model risk continues beyond deployment as foundation models drift and get updated on timelines outside organizational control
- Third-party AI operates on organizational data under someone else's governance, with vendor-embedded agents accessing enterprise systems without appearing in inventories
- The accountability chain for human decisions doesn't exist for AI - most organizations can't identify who approved an agent's decision or was responsible for its policy
- Current governance models assume human operators who stay accountable, but agents now execute transactions autonomously without human oversight
- Most risk leaders cannot confidently reconstruct what happened when an AI agent makes a wrong call due to missing inventory, audit trails, and accountability chains
