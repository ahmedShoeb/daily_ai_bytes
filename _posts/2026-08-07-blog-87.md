## Key Points

- NVIDIA has released Alpamayo 2 Super, an open reasoning model for autonomous vehicles now available for commercial use
- The model is specifically designed to handle complex, rare 'long-tail' driving scenarios that are difficult to anticipate
- Available under the OpenMDW-1.1 permissive license, allowing developers to fine-tune, create derivatives, and use commercially without additional permissions
- The model ranks first on LingoQA autonomous driving reasoning benchmark, outperforming models like GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Qwen2.5-VL 72B
- It offers 360-degree reasoning using full-surround camera coverage to understand lane changes, merges, and complex intersections
- The model provides five tightly coupled outputs: trajectory planning, chain-of-causation traces, meta-actions, auto-labels, and visual question answering
- Can be used as an autolabeler to transform raw driving clips into training data, compressing annotation cycles from months to days
- Part of NVIDIA's broader AV ecosystem including simulation tools (AlpaSim), reinforcement learning (AlpaGym), and physical AI datasets
- Already surpassed 500,000 downloads on Hugging Face, making it the most-adopted open reasoning model for autonomous driving
- Enables a cloud-to-car workflow with frontier-scale reasoning in development and efficient, specialized models in production vehicles
