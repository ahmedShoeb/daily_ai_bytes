## Key Points

- Early AI agents required specific, detailed instructions to avoid doing the wrong thing, but modern AI agents have evolved
- Today's frontier models make mistakes due to incorrect assumptions about user goals and priorities, not confusion
- Modern models are smart enough to understand context and provide meaningful input on broader objectives
- The key recommendation is to share context about your priorities and overall project goals, not just specific tasks
- Example prompt demonstrates sharing both long-term goals (building an AI content detection tool) and short-term objectives (finding efficient models)
- Including context about personal vs. work projects, technical values, and relative priorities helps models make better decisions
- Models can now pick up coding style from surrounding code and adapt their output accordingly
- This approach prevents the 
"XY problem" - asking for technical solutions without giving experts the context they need
- The article argues against seeking "magic prompts" and instead advocates for comprehensive context sharing
