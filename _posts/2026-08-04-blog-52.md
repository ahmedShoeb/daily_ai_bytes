## Key Points

- Opus 5 (AI model) spent ~2 hours and wrote 5500 lines of code to create a 3D procedural render of the first paragraph of Lord of the Rings using a 1M token budget (~$10)
- LLMs enable creation of hyper-custom worlds that would normally be too time-consuming for humans to build, suggesting potential for on-demand gaming experiences
- GPT4.5 has been released and shows 10X more pretraining compute than GPT4, offering subtle but diffuse improvements in creativity, humor, and world knowledge rather than reasoning capabilities
- The model's improvements are mostly in EQ (emotional intelligence) related tasks rather than IQ/reasoning tasks where RL-trained models like o1 remain superior
- The history of the Attention mechanism reveals it was independently developed by Dzmitry Bahdanau in 2014 for machine translation, inspired by how humans shift gaze back and forth during translation
- The name 'attention' was added last-minute by Yoshua Bengio, replacing the original 'RNNSearch' name
- Tokenizers are a completely separate stage of LLM pipelines with their own training, and many LLM quirks trace back to tokenization issues
- 64-year-old predictions from Licklider about computing as intelligence amplification tools are relevant today, though predictions about AI timeline were overly optimistic
- AI has only recently (~last year) started making significant progress into 'thinking' work rather than just automation of rote tasks
