## Key Points

OpenAI has filed confidentially for an IPO but will not go public in 2026, according to CEO Sam Altman
Altman stated that "given everything happening with safety, right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public"
The CEO emphasized that OpenAI is not rushing into an IPO and will go public "when we're ready" which includes when the business is ready and when the societal moment is right for the technology
Altman specifically ruled out 2026 for the IPO, saying "I would say not 2026, yeah. We've got a lot of stuff to do"
The New York Times reported in June 2026 that OpenAI had initially aimed for a Q3 or Q4 2026 IPO but was leaning toward 2027 due to tech stock volatility and the company's financial challenges
The comments were made during an interview with Fortune editor in chief Alyson Shontell amidst discussions about the OpenAI-Hugging Face hack and AI safety concerns
