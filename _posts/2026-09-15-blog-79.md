## Key Points

- Grab is acquiring a controlling 60% stake in Southeast Asian digital lending platform Atome Financial for $1.49 billion in cash
- The deal includes $260 million of primary growth capital and is expected to close by Q3 2027, subject to regulatory approvals
- Grab plans to purchase the remaining 40% stake approximately two years after the initial transaction closes
- The second-stage acquisition price will be performance-based, using a formula tied to Atome's financial results with a valuation floor of $2B and cap of $4.5B
- Atome Financial serves 25 million cumulative users across Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Indonesia and Thailand with BNPL loans, consumer cash loans and other digital lending products
- The company has a $1 billion gross loan portfolio and works with over 30,000 brands
- Grab expects the acquisition to help its Financial Services segment generate $500 million adjusted EBITDA by 2028 with a gross loan portfolio above $6 billion
- The deal will be financed from Grab's existing cash and is expected to be accretive to Group Adjusted EBITDA upon completion
- Grab sees a major financial inclusion opportunity, noting that more than 70% of adults in Southeast Asia are unbanked or underbanked
- The acquisition expands Grab's ecosystem beyond ride-hailing and food delivery into broader financial services across Southeast Asia
