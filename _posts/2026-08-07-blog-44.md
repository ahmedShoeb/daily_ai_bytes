## Key Points

1. Anthropic has reportedly signed a massive $10 billion cloud partnership deal with AI cloud startup Volta over a six-year period
2. Volta, founded earlier this year, will provide cloud compute infrastructure to support Anthropic's Claude AI models
3. The partnership involves crypto-mining company Bitdeer, which will help develop a data center facility in Norway with 133 megawatt capacity
4. The Norway data center will be powered by Nvidia's state-of-the-art Vera Rubin AI chip systems
5. Volta is part of Nvidia's Cloud Partner program - a consortium of AI cloud providers using Nvidia GPUs in their data centers
6. This deal continues Anthropic's recent cloud partnership spree as the company aggressively expands its compute capacity
7. Anthropic has been actively seeking compute deals to compete in the corporate AI race against competitors
8. The company recently announced other compute partnerships with SpaceX and Amazon
9. The information was originally reported by Bloomberg citing anonymous sources familiar with the deal
10. Volta had previously mentioned a deal with an AI lab but hadn't specifically named Anthropic until this report
