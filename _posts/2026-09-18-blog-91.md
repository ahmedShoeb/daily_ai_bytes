## Key Points

- Periodic has developed an AI model called Periodic Neon that outperforms frontier models like GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 on scientific XRD analysis
- Neon achieves 55.3% success rate on FrontierXRD evaluation, a 20x improvement over the initial 2.7% success rate of the open-weight Kimi K2.6 model
- X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis is critical for materials discovery, as it helps identify what phases formed during synthesis experiments
- Traditional XRD analysis can take human experts hours per sample, especially for complex, multiphase materials
- Neon was trained using lab data, reinforcement learning, and midtraining on scientific literature, achieving Pareto-optimal cost-performance
- The model uses 1,300 H200 GPUs for training, significantly less than competitors using 100,000+ Blackwell GPUs
- Periodic developed a specialized scientific harness that outperforms Claude Code + open-source tools by 3.8x on XRD analysis
- LLM-judge ensembles achieved 74.6% agreement with human experts, validating the training approach despite lack of ground-truth labels
- The proprietary midtraining corpus doubles every month and improves downstream RL performance
- Neon demonstrates transferable XRD analysis capabilities that extend to held-out chemical systems beyond training data
