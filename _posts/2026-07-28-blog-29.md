## Key Points

- The paper presents PRIOR, a prototype representation learning framework that jointly learns from medical images and reports
- The approach incorporates both global and local alignment between medical images and reports for fine-grained representation
- A cross-modality conditional reconstruction module is designed to interchange information across modalities by reconstructing masked images and reports
- For reconstructing long reports, a sentence-wise prototype memory bank is constructed to focus on both low-level visual and high-level clinical linguistic features
- The paper proposes a non-auto-regressive generation paradigm for reconstructing non-sequential medical reports
- Experimental results show the method outperforms state-of-the-art methods on five downstream tasks including supervised classification, zero-shot classification, image-to-text retrieval, semantic segmentation, and object detection
- The code for the method is publicly available
- The approach was tested across multiple datasets and under different dataset size settings
