## Key Points

- Liquid AI has released LFM2.5-2.6B, a 2.6B parameter model designed to run entirely on-device for agentic workloads
- The model is small enough to run on phones, fast enough for CPUs, and capable of handling multi-step tasks including planning and tool calling
- Unlike cloud-based agents, this local model offers free inference, low latency, and real privacy - removing per-token costs changes how developers can build
- The model was pre-trained on ~34T tokens and uses a four-stage post-training pipeline: SFT, Teacher Specialization, Multi-Domain On-Policy Distillation, and Agentic RL
- Despite being the smallest model in comparisons, LFM2.5-2.6B often outperforms models 4 times its size on instruction following and tool use benchmarks
- The model achieves 220 tokens/s on Apple M5 Max CPUs and holds 30 tokens/s on phones, making it practical for on-device deployment
- It reaches nearly 15K output tokens per second on a single NVIDIA H100 GPU at high concurrency
- The model ships with day-one support across the inference ecosystem including llama.cpp, MLX, vLLM, SGLang, and ONNX
- Developers can set up local agents in just two steps: serve LFM2.5-2.6B behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, then point agent harnesses at it
- The model is open-weight and available on Hugging Face, designed for high-volume agentic workloads on edge devices where speed, privacy, and local deployment matter
