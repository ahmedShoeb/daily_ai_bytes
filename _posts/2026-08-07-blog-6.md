## Key Points

- Liquid AI has released LFM2.5-2.6B, a 2.6B parameter agentic model designed to run entirely on-device, even on smartphones
- The model enables free inference, low latency, and real privacy by eliminating cloud API dependencies and per-token costs
- LFM2.5-2.6B achieves competitive performance despite being much smaller than comparable models (2.6B vs 4-9B parameters)
- The model outperforms or trades closely with larger models on instruction-following, tool use, and agentic task benchmarks
- It's optimized for speed, decoding 220 tokens/s on M5 Max and 113 tokens/s on Ryzen AI Max+ 395, and even 30 tokens/s on phones
- The training pipeline uses a four-stage approach: Supervised Fine-Tuning, Teacher Specialization, Multi-Domain On-Policy Distillation, and Agentic Reinforcement Learning
- The model ships with day-one support across major inference ecosystems including llama.cpp, MLX, vLLM, and ONNX
- Local agents can be deployed in just two steps: serving the model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint and connecting to agent harnesses
- Both base and post-trained models are available on Hugging Face under open-weight licensing
- The model is particularly suited for high-volume agentic workloads on edge devices where speed, privacy, and local deployment are critical
