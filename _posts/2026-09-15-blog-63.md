## Key Points

- OpenAI has acquired Glass Imaging, a startup specializing in using AI to improve smartphone camera technology
- The acquisition is valued at over $300 million, which is more than three times the company's $100 million valuation from last year's funding round
- The deal has not been officially announced by either company and was reportedly completed quietly in recent months
- Glass Imaging was founded in 2019 by two former Apple engineers and has raised about $30 million from investors
- The company's software, GlassAI, uses neural networks to correct lens aberrations and sensor imperfections at the moment of capture
- The technology aims to bring DSLR-quality images to smartphones and is already being used by companies like Honor in their phones
- OpenAI has been expanding into hardware, including a previous $6.5 billion acquisition of io (led by former Apple design chief Jony Ive)
- OpenAI is reportedly working on a family of devices including a screen-free smart speaker, an AI-focused phone, and earbuds
- Glass Imaging is the latest in a series of OpenAI acquisitions this year, following companies like NextSlide and Northslope
