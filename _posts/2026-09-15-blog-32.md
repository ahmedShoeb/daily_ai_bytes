## Key Points

- Federal prosecutors have accused two Robinhood engineers of insider trading using advance knowledge of crypto token listings
- Engineers Hefu Chai and Huaisong Xiang allegedly bought perpetual futures on Hyperliquid just before Robinhood publicly announced new token listings
- Both defendants reportedly made over $50,000 each from these trades between 2025 and 2026
- The case is notable for involving perpetual futures contracts rather than direct token purchases
- Prosecutors used the Commodity Exchange Act to charge the derivatives trades, treating crypto perpetuals as futures under US law
- This marks a broader legal push to regulate crypto perpetual futures under existing commodities laws
- Both engineers face charges of wire fraud and commodities fraud, with potential sentences of up to召集 to 30 years combined
- The case follows a 2022 precedent where a former Coinbase manager was charged in a similar insider-trading scheme
- Robinhood cooperated with the investigation according to federal prosecutors
