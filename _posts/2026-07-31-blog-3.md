## Key Points

- Mage is a family of lightweight multimodal models from Microsoft built with a fixed 4B-parameter budget
- These models share a 'codec-aligned efficiency philosophy' focused on spending representation capacity where the most meaningful signal exists
- The models are designed to work across both visual understanding and generation tasks
- They are compact enough to train, fine-tune, and deploy on modest hardware
- Despite their small size, they remain competitive with much larger open-source systems
- The architecture emphasizes research-friendliness alongside practical deployment on accessible hardware
