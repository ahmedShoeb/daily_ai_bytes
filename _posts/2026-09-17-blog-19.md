## Key Points

- Anthropic is developing a new "Money" feature for Claude that appears as a separate tab in the mobile app navigation
- The feature allows users to link bank accounts and ask Claude questions about spending, plans, and other financial matters
- It provides Claude with persistent access to financial data, eliminating the need for manual uploads of bank statements
- The feature could analyze spending, identify recurring payments, summarize balances, visualize trends, and help with budgeting
- This aligns with Anthropic's broader push into finance, complementing existing third-party finance connectors like Era Context and PocketSmith
- The closest competitor is ChatGPT's Finances product, which connects accounts through Plaid for US users
- References suggest development is advanced with dedicated navigation and onboarding screens, but launch timeline remains unknown
- Initial rollout would likely be US-only due to banking providers, regulations, and compliance requirements
- Earlier, Anthropic announced ten finance-agent templates for professional work across Claude platforms
- The web interface currently doesn't show the Money tab, suggesting mobile-first development approach
