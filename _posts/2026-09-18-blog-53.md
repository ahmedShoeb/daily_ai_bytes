## Key Points

- Odyssey-3 is a foundation world model that can power robots, drive cars, train AIs, pilot drones, and even play video games
- It's an autoregressive diffusion transformer trained to simulate highly diverse scenarios, capable of controlling physical and virtual systems with the same foundation model
- With only tens of hours of robot demonstrations, Odyssey-3 can learn to control various robot arms and complete complex tasks
- The model shows recovery behaviors absent from training demonstrations, like reorienting grippers and retrieving dropped objects
- Odyssey-3 is collaborating with Flexion for humanoid autonomy and Poke & Wiggle for robot data and benchmarking
- With just 20 hours of simulated driving data, Odyssey-3 can autonomously drive cars on real roads in India
- The model can generate environments where AIs can learn and explore, creating a recursive learning system
- Odyssey-3 can pilot drones using only tens of hours of simulated drone data
- It can play video games like GTA V, Red Dead Redemption 2, and Sleeping Dogs, showing transfer learning between games
- Odyssey-3 represents a leap toward physical agents that understand physics, dynamics, and cause-and-effect like humans do
