## Key Points

- Introduces Dream-RSI, a framework for scalable recursive self-improvement through evolving worlds
- Addresses the exploration bottleneck in autonomous AI agents by making exploration explicit and programmable
- Uses accumulated discovery history as a replay simulator over realized search spaces
- Enables low-cost off-policy feedback for evaluating and refining exploration policies
- Implements a self-improving loop where improved policies drive further discovery
- Applied across algorithm engineering, mathematical optimization, and GPU kernel engineering
- Achieves competitive or improved discovery quality while reducing discovery cost
