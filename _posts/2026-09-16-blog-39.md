## Key Points

- SoftBank Group secured an $11.87 billion loan to support its investment in OpenAI, up from its earlier $10 billion target
- The two-year facility was sealed last week with commitments from around 20 banks
- The financing comes amid concerns about AI development pace and risks, with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman delaying a public offering to address safety concerns
- SoftBank shares tumbled as much as 13% Monday, the largest drop since July 17
- This loan adds to SoftBank's recent debt financing activities including a $10 billion margin loan backed by its OpenAI stake and potential $20 billion bond sale
- SoftBank will repay $25.9 billion of a $40 billion loan obtained earlier this year for OpenAI investment by September 15
- SoftBank is considering a $10-20 billion U.S. dollar junk-bond sale and holding investor meetings in New York this week
- The company has already raised about $37 billion this year from offshore and domestic bond sales and loans
- SoftBank, led by Masayoshi Son, is slated to invest close to $65 billion in OpenAI by October
