## Key Points

Cogent VR-1 is a post-trained cyber reasoning model specialized in multi-domain enterprise attack-chain discovery
VR-1 autonomously investigates enterprise environments, tests hypotheses, crosses system boundaries, and executes attack chains from limited footholds
On Cogent's IntrusionBench benchmark, VR-1 achieved more than 2× lift in pass@3 over the strongest frontier baseline in black-box configuration
IntrusionBench measures whether cyber agents can complete realistic enterprise attacks from limited starting access with execution-based verification
VR-1 demonstrates 'Mythos-class' capability - moving from vulnerability identification to execution-verified attack path development
The model focuses on four key behaviors: investigating under partial information, composing evidence across domains, recovering from dead ends, and verifying actual objectives
In a representative trajectory, VR-1 navigated from CI/CD access through cloud IAM, application runtime, and internal documentation to retrieve encrypted invoice data
VR-1 operates through the Cogent AI Harness which provides tools, environmental state, execution controls, and verification
The model's advantage comes from its ability to find attack paths when they are unknown, rather than just executing known exploits
Current limitations include narrow evaluation scope, variability in success rates, and controlled rather than production-scale environments
