## Key Points

- NVIDIA Alpamayo 2 Super is now available for commercial use as an open reasoning model for autonomous driving
- It's available under OpenMDW-1.1 license on Hugging Face, allowing commercial use, fine-tuning, and redistribution without additional permissions
- The model ranks first on LingoQA autonomous driving reasoning benchmark, outperforming competitors like Qwen2.5-VL 72B, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and GPT-4o
- Alpamayo 2 Super offers 3x the scale of previous Alpamayo models with 10-billion parameters for better generalization from sparse examples
- It provides 360-degree context reasoning using full-surround camera coverage for complex driving scenarios like lane changes, merges, and intersections
- The model produces five tightly coupled outputs: trajectory planning, chain-of-causation traces, meta-actions, reasoning auto-labels, and visual Q&A with 2D visual grounding
- It's part of NVIDIA's growing open ecosystem for AV development including AlpaSim simulation, AlpaGym reinforcement learning, and Physical AI Open Datasets
- The Alpamayo model family has surpassed 500,000 downloads on Hugging Face, making it the most-adopted open reasoning model for autonomous driving
- The open licensing approach allows AV developers to keep control of their data and infrastructure while building specialized models
- The model supports cloud-to-car workflows enabling frontier-scale reasoning in the cloud and efficient real-time inference in production vehicles
