## Key Points

- Prentis, a new AI research lab co-founded by serial entrepreneur Ritankar Das alongside tech veterans Reid Hoffman and Mark Pincus, is in talks to raise $100 million at a $1 billion valuation
- Launched in April 2026, Prentis focuses on training AI models to learn how office workers navigate routine workflows to build agents that can control computers to automate tasks
- The startup has already signed contracts worth up to $50 million with several enterprise customers including healthcare organizations and manufacturers
- Prentis claims its Hive-32B model outperforms OpenAI's GPT-5.4 and Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.6 on computer-use benchmarks for Windows applications
- The company argues its edge comes from running a much smaller, cheaper model with roughly 10 times lower cost per task than frontier APIs
- CEO Ritankar Das, age 31, is also founder of Titan holding company and was UC Berkeley's youngest University Medalist in over a century
- Prentis is betting that automating everyday office tasks will soon outpace coding as AI's biggest use case
- The startup has hired over 25 employees including researchers from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, Tencent, and Alibaba
- Co-founder Reid Hoffman recently stepped down from Microsoft's board to go 'founder mode' on AI drug discovery startup Manas AI
- Co-founder Mark Pincus published a memoir 'Life at the Speed of Play' last month and runs investment firm Reinvent Capital
