## Key Points

- OpenAI has expanded its GPT-6 model lineup with two new models: GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna
- These models are designed to be more cost-efficient versions of the flagship GPT-6 Astra model
- API prices for Sol and Luna have been reduced by 50% compared to their GPT-5.6 promotional pricing
- GPT-6 Sol outperforms Claude Opus 5 at just 9% of the cost per task on AutomationBench (business workflow tests)
- GPT-6 Luna improves on its predecessor by 5.4 percentage points at 58% lower cost per task
- GPT-6 Sol makes about half as many factual mistakes as its predecessor, approaching Astra-level reliability
- Both models show improved performance on coding benchmarks like FrontierCode and DeepSWE v1.1
- GPT-6 Sol and Luna inherit GPT-6 Astra's improved communication style with clearer, more concise answers
- OpenAI has implemented prompt caching improvements that reduce fresh token processing by over 50%
- The models are available in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users
