## Key Points

- Moonshot is openly seeking access to additional NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs to train its next-generation Kimi K4 model, despite US export controls
- The Chinese AI company released its open-source Kimi K3 model in July, featuring 2.8 trillion parameters, multi-modal capabilities, and a 1 million token context window
- Moonshot faces allegations from the Trump administration of using NVIDIA GB300 servers and accessing additional hardware via Thailand to train Kimi K3
- The company is accused of possibly distilling parts of its Kimi K3 model from Anthropic's Fable model, though experts note there wasn't enough time for full distillation
- Microsoft calculates potential $600 million savings by moving Copilot from OpenAI/Anthropic models to Moonshot's Kimi K3
- The Trump administration is currently considering whether to ban open-weight models from China entirely
- Major tech companies including NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, and Dell have jointly petitioned the Trump administration to support open-weight AI model ecosystems
- Moonshot's push for more GPUs for Kimi K4 comes despite political tension and could risk worsening US-China technology conflicts
