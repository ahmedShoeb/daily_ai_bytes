## Key Points

StepAudio 3 Gen is a general-purpose audio generation model that supports multiple audio types in a unified framework, including text-to-speech (TTS), voice design, vocal generation, sound effects, music, and vibe speech
The model departs from the popular diffusion Transformer-based continuous generation approach and instead uses a discrete autoregressive generator over residual vector quantization (RVQ) tokens
It represents general audio at 12.5 Hz in a shared 16 × 2048 residual code space, jointly quantizing both semantic and waveform-level acoustic features
The backbone architecture predicts the first codebook along the time axis using autoregressive modeling, while a lightweight causal Transformer completes the remaining fifteen codebooks along the codebook axis
Three key design principles identified: interference-aware progressive pretraining, RVQ Adaptor for multi-codebook acoustic representations, and discrete autoregressive modeling over shared representations
The model was trained using progressive pretraining, multi-task instruction training, and supervised fine-tuning
StepAudio 3 Gen achieves state-of-the-art performance on both TTS and voice design tasks while maintaining strong capabilities across speech, vocals, sound effects, and music
Audio samples are available at a provided URL for demonstration
The approach represents a significant departure from current trends in audio generation by focusing on discrete token-based modeling rather than continuous diffusion methods
