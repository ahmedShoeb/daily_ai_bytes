## Key Points

Helion Energy has added $35 million to its Series G funding round, bringing the total to $500 million
The fusion company originally announced a $465 million Series G round in June 2023
CEO David Kirtley announced the expanded funding round on LinkedIn, noting new investors include crossover, pension, and sovereign wealth investors
Helion aims to be the first company to commercialize fusion energy and has raised more than $1.5 billion to date
The company recently opened its first office in downtown Seattle as its headcount grows to over 800 employees
Helion has an ambitious agreement with Microsoft to supply energy to a Central Washington data center by 2028
The company broke ground on its 50-megawatt Orion power plant in July 2025
There's uncertainty about whether Helion can meet its 2028 timeline, with Axios reporting full capacity might not be reached until 2030
Scientific publications have questioned Helion's ability to produce sufficient energy from its fusion devices to power the grid
Helion is building a small-scale testbed called 'Tiny Merge' to answer technical questions before finalizing power plant designs
