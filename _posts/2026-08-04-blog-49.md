## Key Points

- DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 is the official release of DeepSeek-V4-Flash, replacing the preview version with substantially enhanced agentic capabilities
- The model has the same structure as DeepSeek-V4-Flash-DSpark and comes with a speculative decoding module attached
- Despite having far fewer activated parameters, it outperforms DeepSeek-V4-Pro (Preview) on various benchmarks and competes with the strongest proprietary models
- The model shows impressive benchmark performance including Terminal Bench 2.1 (82.7), NL2Repo (54.2), Cybergym (76.7), DeepSWE (54.4), and Toolathlon-Verified (70.3)
- Introduces a reasoning_effort parameter with three levels: low, high, and max, which controls how much deliberation the model spends before answering
- For agentic scenarios, recommended sampling parameters are temperature=1.0 with top_p=0.95
- The model can be run with vLLM and SGLang frameworks, with DSpark speculative decoding enabled through specific configurations
- Maximum output length recommendation: 384K tokens for high and max reasoning effort levels
- Model weights are licensed under the MIT License, making it open and accessible for various applications
- The model is available through HuggingFace with extensive documentation and example code for implementation
