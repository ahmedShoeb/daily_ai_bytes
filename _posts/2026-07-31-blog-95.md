## Key Points

Amazon is shifting its AI strategy from a portfolio of specialized models to a single multimodal frontier model
The company plans to deprecate several existing models including Premier, Omni, Canvas (image generation), and Reel (video generation)
Amazon hasn't gained significant market share in the AI model market despite AWS infrastructure success
The new consolidated model will likely still use the Nova brand name
Amazon has made significant investments in AI including $25 billion in Anthropic and $50 billion in OpenAI
AWS revenue grew 28% year-over-year in Q1 2026, showing strong demand for cloud and AI infrastructure
The consolidation may affect upcoming hardware products like an AI-native smartphone with Alexa integration
Amazon recently brought OpenAI's GPT and Codex models to AWS, ending Microsoft's exclusivity
The company reduced headcount within its artificial general intelligence organization
The shift could mean Amazon focuses more on AI infrastructure rather than competing across all AI market segments
