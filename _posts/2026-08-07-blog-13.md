## Key Points

- Backflip AI has developed an AI model that can digitize physical parts into CAD models for about $10 in 1-5 minutes, compared to traditional methods costing $1,500 and taking hours to days
- Most factories don't have digital models for their parts - even major manufacturers like Airbus had to spend hundreds of millions recreating CAD for existing planes
- The company's second-generation AI model constructs parts using native, parametric CAD operations (extrude, revolve, pattern) like human engineers do, creating editable feature trees
- Currently deployed at a top automotive manufacturer where it can digitize half their parts, with plans to handle nearly all parts by year-end
- This transforms broken part situations from emergency 'fire drills' into known processes - pull the file, print or CNC it, and production resumes
- The technology acts as a force multiplier, enabling engineers to direct 70 parts/day instead of 7, and allowing technicians without CAD training to create models
- Available now via Autodesk Fusion add-in or web app, with free accounts offering 4 conversions and plans starting at $20/month
- This represents a practical digital factory solution where every part is editable and manufacturable at your fingertips
