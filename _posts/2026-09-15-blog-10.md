## Key Points

Meta Platforms is developing its own custom AI chips, with third-generation MTIA 450 processors (code-named Arke) currently being tested
The company plans to deploy its internally-designed AI chips in data centers starting in the first half of 2027
Meta's goal is to reduce reliance on outside hardware and lower the enormous energy and computing costs associated with its AI ambitions
The company has committed to deploying more than a gigawatt of these chips over a 12-month period
Meta is prioritizing inference chips (running AI models) rather than combined training/inference chips due to cost concerns
Early results show Arke chips performed within 2-3% of Meta's simulations and have run Meta's models alongside models from DeepSeek and Alibaba
Meta is working with Broadcom on chip design and TSMC on production
The fourth-generation chip, MTIA 500 (Astrid), is expected to complete design work soon and reach data centers by end of 2027
Investors should watch whether custom silicon can materially reduce Meta's AI cost curve and protect margins
The company canceled its planned Olympus processor that was intended to handle both AI training and inference
