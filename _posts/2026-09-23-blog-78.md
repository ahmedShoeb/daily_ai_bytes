## Key Points

OpenAI projects negative free cash flow of $278B between 2026-2030, down from a $305B May estimate
Compute and infrastructure spending target rises sharply to $856B from February's public $600B target
Revenue projected to grow nearly 10x from $36B in 2024 to $350B by 2030
Spending can rise while burn falls because partners like Nvidia and Oracle finance infrastructure build
Nvidia reportedly in talks to guarantee $250B of data center debt using its credit rating
Oracle is spending heavily on data centers for OpenAI commitments, booking costs on its own balance sheet
OpenAI raised $122B in March at $852B valuation but is projected to exhaust funds by 2028
Current talks reportedly value OpenAI at about $1.2 trillion ahead of potential listing
The financing market shows strain as US banks retreated from Oracle's recent $16.3B data center financing
Much of the spending difference between $856B compute and $278B burn is carried by vendors, landlords, and partners
