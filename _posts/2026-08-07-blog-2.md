## Key Points

. Backflip AI has created what they call a "reverse replicator" - an AI model that can quickly digitize physical parts into usable CAD files
- Traditional reverse engineering of broken parts costs about $1,500 per part and takes hours to days, but Backflip's AI does it for about $10 in 1-5 minutes
- Most factories don't have digital models for their own parts - they may have CAD models for less than 1% of components in a typical factory
- The AI model was built from the ground up for 3D geometry and reconstructs parts using actual CAD operations (extrude, revolve, pattern) like human engineers do
- The system is already deployed at a top automotive manufacturer and can automatically digitize half their parts, with near-complete coverage expected by year-end
- This technology transforms factory maintenance from reactive "fire drills" to proactive processes where everything can be quickly recreated when broken
- The web app and Fusion add-in are live today with free accounts offering four conversions and plans starting at $20/month
- This isn't about replacing jobs but multiplying engineering capacity - an engineer who could digitize 7 parts/day can now direct 70
- Even technicians without CAD training can now produce usable models with just a scanner and a click
- The technology represents a fundamental shift in manufacturing economics, making complete factory digitization financially feasible for the first time
