## Key Points

- OpenArm is an open-source 7DOF humanoid arm designed for physical AI research and deployment in contact-rich environments
- Features high backdrivability and compliance, built with safe human-robot interaction in mind while delivering practical payload capabilities
- OpenArm Cell provides standardized environment with unified background, lighting, and camera placement for reproducible research worldwide
- The system features human-scale proportions, safety, compliance, and practical payloads at $6,500 USD for a complete bimanual system
- Designed as a flexible platform for teleoperation, imitation learning, simulation, and real-world data collection
- Project is in continuous development and actively seeking contributors, research partners, and company collaborators
- OpenArm offers both assembled and DIY options with global manufacturer support
- Comprehensive documentation includes hardware designs, ROS2 integration, simulation environments (Isaac Lab, MuJoCo), and dataset tools
- All hardware designs are open-source under CERN-OHL-S-2.0 license with software under various open-source licenses
- Project governance includes a Code of Conduct for all participation
