## Key Points

- Meta has launched Meta One, a comprehensive subscription service with plans for individuals, creators, and businesses
- The service offers over 50 features across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Meta AI, with plans to expand to Edits and AI glasses
- Core and Premium bundles ($7.99-$19.99/mo) combine single app features with expanded AI capabilities like media generation and editing
- Creator and business plans ($14.99-$499/mo) include professional tools, Meta Business Agent access, and advanced analytics
- Single product plans include Instagram Plus ($3.99), WhatsApp Plus ($2.99), and Facebook Plus ($3.99) with app-specific enhancements
- Business features include verified badges, impersonation protection, scheduling tools, and 24/7 customer response capabilities
- Free Meta AI access remains unchanged - subscriptions provide expanded usage of compute-intensive AI tools
- The service launches globally with pricing varying by region, app, and account type
