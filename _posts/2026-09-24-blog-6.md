## Key Points

- Grok 4.7 is x.ai's most capable model specifically designed for coding and knowledge work tasks
- The model works longer on difficult tasks, checks its own work more carefully, and has improved safeguards compared to previous versions
- It's priced competitively at the same cost and speed as Grok 4.6, starting at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens
- Grok 4.7 uses a new, larger base model and was trained with longer reinforcement learning on harder tasks that can take hours to complete
- The model has native understanding of the Grok Bot harness, making it better at conversational tasks and general knowledge work
- It shows significant improvements in creating documents and presentations, performing well on professional benchmarks like GDPval and AA Briefcase
- Features an entirely new safeguard stack with strong refusal and jailbreak resistance, leading in biosafety benchmarks at 62.4%
- Balances strong cybersecurity capabilities with low refusal rates for legitimate security work (only 3.3% of risky prompts allowed through)
- Available today in Cursor and Grok Build, plus through the Grok API, third-party coding harnesses, and cloud platforms
- Offers a fast variant with twice the output speed at twice the price
