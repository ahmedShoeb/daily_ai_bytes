## Key Points

-
Relay, an AI-powered workflow automation tool, shut down after struggling to compete as larger platforms like OpenAI and Google built similar automation into their own tools
-
According to S&P Global Market Intelligence, about 42% of AI initiatives are ultimately abandoned by their corporate parents due to insufficient funding, technical challenges, competition, or weak user demand
-
OpenAI faced criticism and had to roll back a ChatGPT redesign that aimed to turn it into a "super app" with separate Chat, Codex, and Work modes
-
OpenAI has shut down several standalone apps including ChatGPT Atlas (web browser), Operator (AI agent), and its video-sharing platform Sora, folding their features into ChatGPT
-
Apple's improved Siri AI was repeatedly delayed, contributing to a $250 million settlement over marketing claims about iPhone 16 AI capabilities
-
Microsoft's Recall feature faced major privacy backlash for creating searchable archives of user activity, leading to a year-long delay and ongoing security concerns
-
Notion Mail (AI-focused email product) is shutting down as users preferred using separate AI agents for email management
-
Humane AI Pin failed despite raising $230 million, with performance issues and battery fire risks leading to business shutdown and acquisition by HP
-
Rabbit R1 sold 100,000 units initially but was criticized as unfinished and unreliable, though the company continues to update it and work on new hardware
-
Other notable failures include Huxe (AI audio app), Yupp (AI model comparison playground), and Figgs AI (character role-play platform)
