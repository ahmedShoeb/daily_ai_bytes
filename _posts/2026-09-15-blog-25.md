## Key Points

- vLLM achieved 2.8× throughput improvement for Kimi K3 models through comprehensive performance optimizations
- Average latency dropped by 55-60% across different concurrency levels (1 to 16 concurrent requests)
- Throughput increased by up to 180.6% with 16 concurrent requests, reaching 725 tokens/second
- First token time (TTFT) was reduced by 83-85% at concurrency 1 and 16 respectively
- Multiple technical optimizations were implemented including adaptive speculative-token budgets and internal KDA prefix checkpoints
- ReplaySSM technique reconstructs KDA state instead of storing it, increasing effective cache capacity by 11%
- Zero-copy mixed KDA batches eliminated data movement operations, boosting throughput by 5.2-7.7%
- Decode context parallelism increased KV-cache capacity from 1.93M to 19.75M tokens for long-sequence workloads
- All optimizations maintained model accuracy with negligible impact on GSM8K benchmark scores
- The improvements came from a community-driven effort tracked in GitHub issue #50587 with contributions from multiple engineers and researchers
