## Key Points

- Magic's pretraining recipe is now over 10x more compute-efficient than leading open-weight base models
- The company matched DeepSeek V4 Pro Base performance using approximately 50x fewer FLOPs, costing only ~$0.5M on GB200
- They outperformed all publicly available open base models on perplexity evals after scaling 10x to ~$4M compute budget
- The research focused on pretraining efficiency as part of Magic's three pillars: pretraining, agentic RL, and long-context capabilities
- The team developed detailed evaluation methods including bits-per-byte loss measurements and scaling law projections
- They created comprehensive evaluation sets covering 167 domains with special attention to coding, research, and reasoning tasks
- Magic discovered that many techniques that work on tiny models don't scale to large models, requiring careful scaling approach
- The company is likely the smallest team in the world training trillion-parameter models
- Next steps include scaling long-horizon RL, alignment training research, and model release preparations
- The research demonstrates significant algorithmic efficiency gains as an alternative to brute-force scaling with massive compute clusters
