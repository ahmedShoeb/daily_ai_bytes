## Key Points

- Shieldstral is a 3B open-weights multimodal safety classifier that uses natural language policies at inference time instead of fixed taxonomies
- The model takes a unique approach where users write safety policies as plain-language questions at inference time, and it returns calibrated safety scores
- Shieldstral matches or outperforms open guard models up to 7x its size on text safety benchmarks and sets new state-of-the-art for multimodal moderation
- The model unifies text, image, and text+image content moderation across prompts, responses, and prompt-response pairs into a single interface
- Mistral AI is releasing Shieldstral as open weights under Apache 2.0 as part of the Open Secure AI Alliance with NVIDIA and other organizations
- The model solves several data challenges: unifying heterogeneous safety datasets, teaching policy discrimination rather than memorization, and grounding safety in images
- Shieldstral runs on a single 16GB GPU, making it practical for deployment
- The development was built end-to-end on Mistral's Forge platform for training and evaluating custom models
- This approach allows safety policies to adapt to specific products, audiences, and contexts rather than using a one-size-fits-all taxonomy
- Mistral is continuing work on multilingual coverage, longer-document robustness, and broader multimodal safety features
