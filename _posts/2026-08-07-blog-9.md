## Key Points

- Cursor has open-sourced Mixture-of-Kittens (MoK), a deterministic mixture-of-experts (MoE) training megakernel designed specifically for NVL72 systems
- MoK addresses the bottleneck of MoE layers in training their Composer coding model by fusing all communication and computation into a single kernel
- The kernel achieved 1.41x end-to-end tokens per second improvement in their production training stack across several NVL72 racks
- Key innovations include choosing pull-based communication for better scheduling and NVLink bandwidth utilization, optimal computation-communication overlap granularity, and eliminating CPU-GPU synchronization with ring token buffers
- MoK supports both BF16 and MXFP8 precision modes and includes determinism for reproducible training results
- Benchmarks show MoK is up to 2.37x faster than publicly available implementations for MXFP8 forward pass and delivers significant speedups across various model shapes
- The development leveraged AI agents to automate simpler tasks and help navigate complex distributed system challenges
- This open-source release aims to lower barriers to AI research by making high-performance MoE training more accessible
