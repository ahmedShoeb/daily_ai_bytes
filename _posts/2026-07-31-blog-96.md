## Key Points

- Fish Audio has launched S2.1 Pro as its recommended production voice model designed for real-time conversational speech rather than scripted narration
- The model offers a time-to-first-audio latency of around 90 milliseconds, fast enough for natural turn-taking in live dialogue
- It supports an impressive 83 languages with a single voice identity that remains consistent across all languages
- The model uses free-form bracket tags within text to control delivery style (like whispers or nervous laughs) without preset emotion menus
- S2.1 Pro supports multi-speaker dialogue and voice cloning from short reference samples (10-30 seconds) without additional fine-tuning
- It includes a free tier for development and testing with the same model quality and language coverage as the paid version
- The model is built as an improvement over the earlier S2-Pro with better quality, latency, and throughput
- Fish Audio's open-source Fish Speech project has gained significant traction with over 20,000 stars on GitHub
- The company positions S2.1 Pro for agent workflows, voice agents, phone systems, and long-form audio pipelines
- The model was trained on large multilingual audio datasets and achieves low word error rates by company benchmarks
