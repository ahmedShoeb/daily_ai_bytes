## Key Points

- Cognition, developer of coding assistant Devin, raised $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation
- The round was led by top-tier VCs including Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst, and Avenir
- This valuation comes just four months after Cognition's previous fundraise at $26 billion valuation
- Cognition's annualized run-rate revenue grew from $492 million to $900 million since May
- The startup leases an expensive Nvidia server cluster costing hundreds of millions annually
- Cognition could face cash burn of $800 million this year
- Unlike competitor Cursor (which sold to SpaceX), Cognition is training its own model to reduce reliance on expensive third-party models
- Cognition is expected to reach $4-5 billion in annualized revenue by end of 2026
- Major enterprise customers include Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, and Citi
- Founder Scott Wu is a math prodigy who started the company in 2024
