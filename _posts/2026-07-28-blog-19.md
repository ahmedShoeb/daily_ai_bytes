## Key Points

CTVIS (Consistent Training for Online Video Instance Segmentation) is a novel training strategy for online video instance segmentation
The approach aligns training and inference pipelines by constructing contrastive items more effectively
CTVIS builds contrastive items using momentum-averaged embeddings and memory bank storage mechanisms
The method adds noise to relevant embeddings for more robust training
This enables reliable comparison between current instance embeddings and stable representations of historical instances
CTVIS effectively addresses VIS challenges including occlusion, re-identification, and deformation
The approach outperforms state-of-the-art VIS models by up to +5.0 points on benchmarks
Achieves 55.1% AP on YTVIS19, 50.1% AP on YTVIS21, and 35.5% AP on OVIS benchmarks
The research demonstrates that pseudo-videos transformed from images can train robust models surpassing fully-supervised ones
