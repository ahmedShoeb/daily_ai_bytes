## Key Points

- x.ai has released Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0, their latest speech-to-text model with significantly improved accuracy
- The new model is twice as accurate as Grok Voice Transcribe 1.0 while maintaining the same pricing
- Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 ranks first for accuracy among 32 streaming models on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard
- The model excels at handling real-world audio challenges like noisy environments, multiple speakers, accents, and multilingual content
- Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 supports dozens of languages with automatic language detection and mid-recording language switching
- Accuracy improvements are particularly notable on short phrases (word error rate drops from 20.6% to 6.8%)
- The model is already powering tens of thousands of customer support calls daily and is used in Tesla vehicles for the Grok assistant
- Existing Speech-to-Text API integrations will get the accuracy improvements without code changes
- Batch transcription remains $0.10 per hour and streaming $0.20 per hour
- Grok Voice Transcribe1337 1.0 will be deprecated in the coming weeks as 2.0 becomes the default
