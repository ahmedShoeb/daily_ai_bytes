## Key Points

- Meta is developing a dedicated Mail tab for its Muse AI agent that would provide the agent with its own separate mailbox
- The unreleased interface suggests users could review Muse's email conversations in one centralized place
- The feature could serve multiple purposes: Muse might get its own email address or aggregate communication from users' connected personal accounts
- The functionality aligns well with Meta's upcoming Shared Agents feature, allowing businesses to use agents as reusable templates with dedicated mailboxes
- Muse recently reached the top spot on the US App Store, though availability remains limited outside the US
- This addition fits Meta's vision of Muse as a personal agent capable of handling tasks across connected services
- Meta's desktop app with computer-use capabilities was recently released, showing the platform's expansion
- No confirmed release date exists for the Mail tab, and its exact functionality remains unclear
