## Key Points

- Spotify CEO Daniel Ek discussed AI-powered features during the company's Q2 earnings call, focusing on personalization, podcast summarization, and AI-generated ads.
- The company's existing AI DJ feature has been successful, and Ek hinted at more AI features to contextualize and personalize content across the platform.
- One potential use of generative AI would be to summarize podcasts to make it easier for users to discover new content, which could lead to higher engagement.
- Ek mentioned that AI-generated audio ads could significantly reduce advertising costs by allowing advertisers to create and test thousands of ad variations.
- Spotify is pursuing an AI-powered text-to-speech synthesis patent that can create human-like speech with emotional nuance, which could be used for audiobook narration.
- The company acquired AI voice startup Sonantic in 2022 to power its AI DJ feature, showing continued investment in AI voice technology.
- Spotify reported strong growth with 220 million paying subscribers (up 17% year-over-year) and 551 million monthly active users overall.
- Despite revenue growth of 11% year-over-year to €3.2 billion ($3.5 billion), the company reported an operating loss of €247 million ($274 million).
- Spotify recently announced price hikes for its premium plans in the U.S., with individual plans increasing from $9.99 to $10.99 per month.
