## Key Points

- Kimi K3 is a scaled-up production version of their Kimi Linear model released last year, growing from 48B to 2.8 trillion parameters, making it the biggest open-weight model currently
- New LatentMoE component is similar to Nemotron 3 Ultra's LatentMoE and works by compressing (down-projecting) large linear layers
- Kimi K3 follows the trend of other frontier models (Nemotron 3, DeepSeek V4) toward better inference efficiency through component replacements
- Attention residuals improve the residual path by connecting residuals across layers using attention scores for weighting contributions, adding 4% training cost and 2% inference cost
- Kimi K3 completely replaces RoPE layers with NoPE (No Positional Embeddings) everywhere, being the first frontier-level model to do so
- Model features native multimodal support, marking a significant advancement from previous versions
- Architecture focuses on efficiency tweaks including MoE to LatentMoE and regular attention to multi-head latent attention and Kimi Delta Attention
