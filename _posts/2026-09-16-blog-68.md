## Key Points

,Claude's Fable 5.1 successfully solved the 370-year-old Cyphral Distich cipher that had baffled cryptographers since 1899
The cipher consists of two lines of 32 numbers each from Sir Thomas Urquhart's 1653 work Logopandecteision
Previous attempts failed because they looked for external cipher alphabets, while the key was actually in the book itself
The solution method: use the i-th number as a word index in the i-th Proquiritation paragraph and take the first letter
The deciphered message reveals a Royalist prayer: 'O GOD UPHOLD KING CHARLS THE SECOND AND / MAKE HIM THE SUPREME RULER OF THIS LAND'
Fable also solved Urquhart's larger Cyphral Octastich (285 numbers) using the same principle - pages instead of paragraphs
The octastich reveals a longer royalist prayer written in ottava rima form, dated March 1652
Fable spent 44 minutes and 176k tokens to solve it with minimal human guidance
The solution demonstrates AI's ability to solve historical mysteries by combining persistence with pattern recognition
This shows how AI can overcome human attention bottlenecks in solving obscure historical puzzles
