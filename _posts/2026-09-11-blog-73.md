## Key Points

- Meta has introduced Muse, a secure, private personal AI agent that proactively helps with people's goals and suggests ideas
- Muse runs on a dedicated virtual machine called Muse Secure VM that houses both the agent and user data, creating a new kind of secure computer
- The agent works like messaging another person and can handle tasks from simple actions like sending emails to complex goals requiring coordinated planning
- Muse can work autonomously - opening browsers, filling out forms, negotiating on behalf of users, and continuing work even after the app is closed
- It features integrated payment systems with Link (by Stripe) including purchase protections and one-time-use cards for secure transactions
- Muse remembers user preferences and can make unprompted suggestions based on saved items like Instagram recipe reels
- Built with unprecedented privacy features: runs on isolated dedicated computers, has a separate Sentinel agent for oversight, doesn't see passwords/payment details
- Users maintain full control over what Muse can access and do, with complete audit trails and the ability to disconnect services anytime
- Coming soon: Muse Confidential VM where everything is encrypted with user-held keys, making data inaccessible even to Meta
- Muse is rolling out free in the US with subscription tiers for advanced features, available on iOS, Android, and muse.ai website
