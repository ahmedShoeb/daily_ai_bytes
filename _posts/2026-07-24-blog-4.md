## Key Points

- Mage is a family of lightweight, research-friendly multimodal models built at a fixed 4B-parameter budget
- The family includes Mage-VL (for image & video understanding) and Mage-Flow (for text-to-image generation & editing)
- Both models are compact enough to train, fine-tune, and deploy on modest hardware while remaining competitive with larger systems
- Mage-Flow features an efficient tokenizer (Mage-VAE) that matches FLUX.2-VAE fidelity using ~12× fewer encode MACs per pixel
- The model supports native resolution from 512 to 2048 on any aspect ratio, including extreme 4:1 ratios
- System optimizations provide ~2.5× faster training speed and real-time generation on single A100 GPUs
- Mage-Flow-Edit supports semantic content editing, appearance transformation, and image restoration in a unified model
- All checkpoints are available on Hugging Face with Base, RL-aligned, and 4-step Turbo variants
- The models are released for research purposes only with responsible AI considerations incorporated throughout development
- The project is released under the MIT License with emphasis on controlled research settings with human oversight
