## Key Points

- Vercel reduced its inbound sales development team from 10 people to just 1.25 people through AI automation
- The company achieved 90% automation of sales development for inbound leads using AI
- Vercel's home-built support agent handles 93% of all support cases
- The total annual infrastructure cost for both sales development and support automation is in the single-digit thousands of dollars
- The sales development agent delivers a 32x return on investment (ROI)
- AI now functions as a '99th percentile performer 99% of the time' according to Vercel's COO
- The automation success parallels self-driving car technology improvements, similar to Waymo's 92% reduction in pedestrian collisions
- The article positions AI as finally delivering on its initial promises for sales development automation
