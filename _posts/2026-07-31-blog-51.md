## Key Points

 (. Anthropic researchers used Claude Mythos Preview to discover improved attacks on cryptographic algorithms
- The first attack significantly weakens HAWK, a post-quantum digital signature scheme candidate that was undergoing NIST standardization
- Mythos cut HAWK's effective key strength in half by finding a previously unexploited symmetry (nontrivial automorphism) in its lattice structure
- The second attack improved cryptanalysis of round-reduced AES-128 (7 rounds instead of full 10), making attacks 200-800 times faster
- Neither attack affects production systems - HAWK is not deployed, and the AES attack only works on a reduced version
- Mythos discovered these flaws autonomously with minimal human intervention, costing about $100,000 in API costs per attack
- The HAWK attack was discovered in 60 hours of work, while the AES attack required about one week of autonomous work by Claude
- This demonstrates frontier AI models' potential to find mathematical flaws in cryptographic algorithms, not just implementation bugs
- Anthropic partnered with academic institutions to create CryptanalysisBench, a benchmark for evaluating LLMs' cryptanalytic capabilities
