## Key Points

- Anthropic has signed a $10 billion deal with AI cloud startup Volta for cloud compute services
- The six-year agreement involves Volta providing cloud infrastructure to support Anthropic's Claude AI models
- Volta is partnering with crypto-mining company Bitdeer to develop a data center in Norway with 133 megawatt capacity
- The facility will use Nvidia's state-of-the-art Vera Rubin AI chip architecture
- Volta is part of Nvidia's Cloud Partner program, a consortium of AI cloud providers using Nvidia GPUs
- Anthropic has been aggressively expanding its compute capacity through multiple cloud partnerships
- The company recently announced similar compute deals with SpaceX and Amazon
- This move is part of Anthropic's corporate battle for competitive advantage in the AI space
