## Key Points

- Vercel has released DeepsecBench, a new benchmark for evaluating how well AI models find cybersecurity vulnerabilities in application code.
- The benchmark tests models on 50 entry-point files with a golden set of 231 human-judged findings, scoring based on recall-weighted F2 metrics.
- Frontier models from OpenAI and Anthropic score highest, but open-weight models like Kimi K3 and Grok 4.5 offer better cost-performance ratios.
- Kimi K3 achieves half the top score for about one-fifth of the cost, while Grok 4.5 offers near-Kimi performance for less than half the cost.
- The benchmark reveals that higher price doesn't guarantee proportionally better performance, with GPT-5.6 Sol providing the best score-to-cost balance among top models.
- Security scanning costs vary dramatically: a full pass on a production codebase ranges from ~$1,200 with Kimi K3 to over $5,000 with frontier models.
- Organizations can build multi-model scanning programs using frontier models for deep audits and cheaper models for frequent scans.
- The benchmark runs through AI Gateway, providing automatic routing, retries, and failover without per-provider key management.
- Defenders have an advantage over attackers because they can see their entire system, making the same AI models more powerful for internal security.
- The benchmark stays secret to prevent models from memorizing fixes - the best run finds only 30.7% of vulnerabilities, showing real analysis is needed.
