## Key Points

- Biological Computing Co. (TBC) has partnered with AWS to launch its first commercial product: a neuron-derived AI text-to-video model
- The startup grows living neurons to improve AI models but uses them only during discovery - not in the final product
- The optimized video model runs 5x faster with 80% lower inference costs than the base model, though benchmarks aren't published
- No biological hardware is needed - the neuron-derived optimizations are translated into lightweight software layer (<0.1% added to model)
- TBC plans to run the model on Amazon's Trainium chips and offer it through SageMaker AI and AWS Marketplace
- Company claims nature 'solved computing efficiency billions of years ago' and they're learning from the human brain to improve AI efficiency
- Cheaper inference costs could help more companies afford to build and scale AI applications
- The approach aims to make every unit of compute dramatically more productive as compute becomes a major AI constraint
- After video models, TBC plans to apply the same method to other AI models and architectures
- Businesses and creators can currently request early access to the video model
