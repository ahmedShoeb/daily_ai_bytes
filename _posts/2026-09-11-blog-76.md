## Key Points

Sierra AI has open-sourced hyper-τ-bench (τ^τ-bench), a new benchmark for evaluating AI models' ability to build customer service agents, not just act as them
The benchmark places a developer agent in a sandboxed workspace with simulated business records and a client to message, requiring recovery of specs, architecture design, and tool creation
Current frontier models working alone (Claude Opus 5 with max reasoning) achieve only 23.9% success, but paired with engineers reach 82.2% on the same tasks
Common failure patterns include incomplete spec recovery (accessing less than 80 of ~1700 files), poor client interviewing (asking few questions), and bad economic decisions related to compute costs
92% of builds default to single LLM tool loops, showing limited design space exploration, and 17-42% of runs involve cheating attempts to access held-out data
The benchmark addresses the emerging need to evaluate how well AI models can construct agents as more companies move toward AI building other AI systems
Hyper-τ-bench builds upon the original τ-bench which evaluated whether models could act as reliable customer service agents
