## Key Points

- AI companies' intellectual property moat is very shallow despite massive investments in model training
- Models can be replicated through 'distillation' - repeatedly querying AI systems to extract their capabilities
- Elon Musk testified that xAI used distillation techniques on OpenAI models, calling it common industry practice
- Anthropic accused Chinese AI labs of creating thousands of fake accounts to distill Claude's capabilities
- Distillation is affordable because victims subsidize their services, making attacks economically feasible
- The security vulnerabilities in IoT devices allow for massive botnets that could be used for distillation attacks
- AI companies face two choices: implement anti-distillation defenses or raise prices to make attacks uneconomic
- Closed models are already on the flattening part of the technology S-curve, making catching up easier for competitors
- Open-weight models are narrowing the capability gap with closed models and sometimes exceed them in specific areas
- The shallow moat doesn't justify trillion-dollar valuations, massive data center investments, or space ambitions
