## Key Points

- Apple announced a new iCloud+ subscription bundle that includes Apple TV and Apple Arcade at no additional cost in over 100 countries
- The bundle applies to all paid iCloud+ plans (including the cheapest 50GB tier) and extends via family sharing to up to five people
- This initiative excludes major markets like the US, Canada, Australia, UK, and other European countries
- In affected countries, Apple TV and Apple Arcade will no longer be sold as individual subscriptions - existing standalone subscriptions will be cancelled during transition
- Apple One is being effectively wound down in these markets, with new customers unable to subscribe, signaling a shift away from Apple One toward iCloud+
- Existing iCloud+ subscribers automatically gain access to TV and Arcade, while individual TV/Arcade subscribers will be moved to iCloud+
- Apple also includes Apple Music Select (ad-free curated music stations) as part of the bundle to entice upgrades to full Apple Music
- Apple TV+ is expanding to 59 new countries, making the streaming service available in 170 countries worldwide
- The announcement comes from Eddy Cue, Apple's senior vice president of Services and Health
- This represents a strategic shift in Apple's services packaging for smaller and emerging markets
