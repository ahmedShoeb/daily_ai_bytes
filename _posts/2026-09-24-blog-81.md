## Key Points

. The article discusses how AI is being used to optimize the most basic programming primitive: the IF-THEN statement
. New specialized AI models like Jev and SemIf can make decisions in hundreds of milliseconds at a 99% reduction in cost compared to traditional AI models
. These optimized models run the attention math once and determine probabilities of allowed answers based on a few tokens of output, rather than generating full text
. In the author's own testing, replacing traditional AI calls with these specialized deciders nearly doubled classification accuracy from 47% to over 80% on email thread classification
. This represents nearly two orders of magnitude in cost reduction while achieving higher accuracy
. The article suggests this is the beginning of AI specialization for production workflows, creating a bifurcation between state-of-the-art models for discovery/training and optimized models for production execution
. The economic implication is that 'harnesses' (systems that cut execution cost without sacrificing accuracy) will capture significant margin as AI primitives become specialized
