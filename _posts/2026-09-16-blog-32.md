## Key Points

- Between September 1-3, three major AI labs (Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI) each launched two versions of their latest models: a public version for sale and a vetted version with full capabilities requiring application/approval
- The public tier prices remained standard ($10/$50 per million tokens for Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, $0.75/$3.75 for Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash until year-end)
- The vetted tiers have no listed price and require identity/organizational verification: OpenAI's Daybreak Blue uses government ID + hardware keys, Anthropic's Mythos requires organizational ID, Google's Flash Cyber goes to specific institution categories
- OpenAI's public Astra tier includes monitors that can stop long-running agent tasks on the API, directly impacting solo builders running automated workloads
- The 'same model, different safeguards' approach means benchmark results from vetted tiers don't reflect public model performance, creating a gap based on user status rather than payment
- Solo developers must consider which tier their products depend on, apply for access early if needed, and build resilience for API task interruptions
