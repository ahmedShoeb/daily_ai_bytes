## Key Points

- Apple has reversed its stance on using customer data to train its AI models with iOS 27
- Previously, Apple claimed it didn't need customer data to make Siri AI work well
- A new setup screen asks users to opt in to "improve Siri and Apple Intelligence" with options to "Share Audio and Text" or "Not Now"
- Apple maintains it "takes steps to ensure that personal data is not stored or used by Apple" but audio material may be reviewed by Apple employees after anonymization
- The collected data may be reviewed by "review personnel" (unclear if Apple employees or third-party contractors)
- iOS 26 had a single toggle to disable all Apple Intelligence features, but this was removed in iOS 27 requiring users to turn off each AI feature individually
- The type of data collected isn't changing, but Apple will now use it to train and refine its Foundation Models as well as Siri and Dictation
- The article questions whether on-device models now always take up space on internal hard drives since the single toggle to disable Apple Intelligence was removed
