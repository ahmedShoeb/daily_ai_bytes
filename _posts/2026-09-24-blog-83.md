## Key Points

- Anthropic is quietly testing its upcoming frontier model Claude Fable 5.2 in the wild, just weeks after launching Claude Fable 5.1
- Users reported receiving much more advanced responses than typical Claude outputs, suggesting a newer, more advanced model
- Examples include a ship-shooter game where Fable 5.2 created both the game and video demo using pure JavaScript without external files or plugins
- The model produces interactive canvas scenes with smooth animations where animated characters track mouse cursor movements
- Anthropic appears to be directing certain prompts through the web interface while rolling out changes like merging Cowork and chat for paid subscribers
- Users are comparing Fable 5.2 outputs with OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and reporting it holds its own against the OpenAI product
- Anthropic also has an upcoming Opus release reportedly jumping to version 5.5, rumored to be announced this week
- It's unclear whether Anthropic will release the new models broadly or continue quiet testing
- The article notes that versioning and timelines are subject to change
