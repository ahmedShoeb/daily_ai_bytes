## Key Points

- Xiaomi has released and open-sourced its MiMo-V2.6 series, featuring two natively omnimodal models designed for coding, visual tasks, and computer use
- MiMo-V2.6-Pro is the flagship model with high performance, while MiMo-V2.6-Flash offers a lower-cost balance of intelligence and efficiency
- The Pro-UltraSpeed version provides up to 20 times faster output at the same quality for latency-sensitive applications
- MiMo-V2.6-Pro scored 46.32 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3, reportedly outperforming competitors like Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8 Max
- API pricing remains at V2.5 levels: Flash at $0.14/million input tokens and $0.28 output, Pro at $0.435 and $0.87, with UltraSpeed costing ten times more
- Xiaomi trained the models using reinforcement learning on complex tasks, completing 30 RL steps across 750,000 trajectories in under six days
- The training spanned coding, general-agent, visual, and cybersecurity tasks using context lengths up to one million tokens
- Beyond traditional software work, the models can create interactive 3D scenes, control robotic arms, produce frontends, compose music, and handle advanced scientific tasks
- Both models are available in AI Studio, MiMo Code, MiMo Desktop, Xiaomi's API Platform and OpenRouter, with the technical report and training code also being published
