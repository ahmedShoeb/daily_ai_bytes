## Key Points

- The Open Secure AI Alliance brings together over 80 tech leaders including NVIDIA, Adobe, Microsoft, Google, and many others to create open AI security tools
- The alliance aims to develop open AI security models, harnesses, and tools that defenders can study, adapt, and deploy on their own infrastructure
- The initiative was inspired by the recent Hugging Face security incident where closed AI tools blocked forensic analysis, forcing them to use an open-weight model to analyze 17,000+ actions
- The alliance argues that open AI systems are essential for cybersecurity because they democratize defensive capabilities and create transparency for defenders
- NVIDIA is contributing open models, model weights, and new agent harness research including the Object-Oriented Agent (NOOA) project
- Other major contributions include HPE's work on zero-trust identity frameworks, Hugging Face's Safetensors format, and Microsoft's MDASH multi-model scanning harness
- The alliance warns policymakers against blanket restrictions on open AI systems, arguing this would weaken defensive capabilities and create single points of failure
- The initiative positions open AI security tools as defensive assets rather than liabilities in cybersecurity policy
