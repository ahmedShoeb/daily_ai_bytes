## Key Points

Meta is introducing a new subscription service called Meta One with plans for individuals, creators, and businesses
Core free experiences across Meta apps and Meta AI remain unchanged - subscriptions offer specialized capabilities and expanded AI usage
Meta One launches with over 50 features on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Meta AI, with expansion to Edits and AI glasses planned
Individual plans include single product subscriptions ($2.99-3.99/month) and bundle plans ($7.99-19.99/month) for AI power users
Business and creator plans range from $14.99 to $499/month with professional tools and increased Meta Business Agent access
Key features include more AI usage (image/video generation with Muse models), Restyle tool on Instagram, and customization options
Creator/business tools include enhanced profiles, follow invitations, verified badges, scheduling capabilities, and analytics
More than half of early test subscribers engaged with both AI and expression features in bundled plans
Meta One is now available globally with pricing varying by region, app, and account type
