## Key Points

- Anthropic has released Salesforce in Claude in beta, a plugin that integrates Salesforce CRM capabilities directly into Claude AI
- The plugin includes 37 skills for daily sales tasks like account research, call preparation, pipeline review, and CRM updates
- Claude can automatically assemble information scattered across Salesforce, email, call recordings, and Slack to save sellers hours of administrative work
- The plugin features two connectors: Salesforce connector for reading/updating data and Slack connector for deal-channel summaries and team threads
- Each morning, Claude delivers personalized briefs with daily meetings, closing deals, at-risk opportunities, and unread threads
- For call preparation, Claude pulls briefs from multiple sources and can add missing stakeholders to Salesforce as contacts
- Claude can analyze deals, score them against team methodology, draft business cases, and create mutual close plans
- After meetings, Claude can turn transcripts/notes into follow-up emails, Slack summaries, and draft Salesforce opportunity updates
- The plugin builds interactive dashboards showing pipeline coverage, risk factors, and can generate forecast narratives for leadership
- The integration respects existing Salesforce permissions and requires seller approval for changes by default
