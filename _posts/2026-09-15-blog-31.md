## Key Points

- Agility Robotics has unveiled the Digit 5, a humanoid robot designed specifically for safe operation near human coworkers
- The robot features an advanced safe motion system that can detect human presence and take autonomous precautions
- When detecting people at a distance, Digit 5 can move to avoid them or stand still to allow safe passage
- In close proximity situations, the robot can squat into a seated position to reduce risk
- This safety-focused design aims to eliminate the need for isolated robot work cells and physical separation barriers in workplaces
- Agility plans to offer early access to Digit 5 in the first half of 2027, with general availability by the end of that year
- The company is retooling its RoboFab facility in Salem, Oregon to manufacture the new robots
- Previous versions of Digit robots have already logged over 65,000 hours of operation in warehouses and factories across North America
- Agility was the first to deploy humanoid robots in full-time commercial operations at a GXO warehouse in Atlanta in 2024
- The company has worked with clients including GXO, Schaeffler, Amazon, and Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada
