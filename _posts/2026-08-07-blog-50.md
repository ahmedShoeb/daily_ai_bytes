## Key Points

 - CrushBank transformed from an IT support business into an enterprise-wide data and AI solutions company using IBM Bob
 - IBM Bob helped solve the common enterprise AI challenge of accessing valuable data trapped in legacy systems without modern APIs
 - The company reduced proof-of-concept development time from weeks with 4-person teams to single afternoons with one developer and IBM Bob
 - IBM Bob assists with analyzing legacy systems, generating ingestion code, and building MCP servers that connect data to AI models like Claude, ChatGPT, and Llama
 - The human-in-the-loop approach ensures developers maintain control and review all generated plans, code, and queries for safety and correctness
 - CrushBank's architecture includes IBM Cloud, watsonx.data, Iceberg, Milvus vector database, Presto, and Spark for enterprise-grade data management
 - The platform supports multiple AI models, allowing customers to use their preferred AI assistants while maintaining data flexibility
 - IBM Bob helped CrushBank build a churn prediction model by combining data from project systems, ERP, and CRM to identify at-risk customers
 - The company maintains strict development discipline with code reviews, sensitive data scanning, and human review before production deployment
 - The approach has expanded CrushBank's market from IT support AI to broader enterprise data enablement for any company with legacy data
