## Key Points

- Ungoverned AI is the primary risk that compounds all other AI risks, embedded in existing software and deployed by business units without proper review
- Agentic AI breaks traditional monitoring tools since agents can chain actions across systems faster than human review cycles can track
- Model risk doesn't end at deployment - foundation models drift and get updated on timelines enterprises don't control
- Third-party AI operates on enterprise data under someone else's governance program, with vendor-embedded agents accessing systems without appearing in inventory
- Traditional accountability chains for human decisions don't exist for AI decisions - most organizations can't identify who approved an agent's decision or was responsible for its policy
- Most risk leaders lack confidence in reconstructing AI agent mistakes because necessary inventory, audit trails, and accountability chains were never built for autonomous AI
- Current governance models assume human operators remain accountable, but this assumption is gone with agents acting across systems without human oversight
- Existing management platforms were built for data privacy and compliance documentation, not runtime AI behavior monitoring
- Boards and regulators are asking questions that most organizations currently cannot answer about AI risk and accountability
