## Key Points

- OpenAI has introduced a new AI-native ad format for ChatGPT that opens conversations with brands instead of sending users to websites
- When users click the 'Chat with us' button, they're taken to a branded chat within ChatGPT where they can interact with the brand's AI agent
- This format moves beyond basic ads under ChatGPT responses to become an entry point for brand conversations
- Wayfair is currently testing this format as part of OpenAI's Sponsored Agent pilot program
- The format is similar to Meta Ads that send people to WhatsApp for opening communication lines with prospects
- OpenAI's CFO Sarah Friar described current ads as the 'basic starting point' and said they're seeing early indications of more AI-native ad formats
- Advertisers are still determining ChatGPT's value as an ad platform, with ROI remaining unproven for many brands
- The new format could help OpenAI address ROI concerns by offering an in-platform shopping experience
- Retailers face a trade-off between keeping customers in ChatGPT versus collecting data through their own sites
- This represents OpenAI's search for a signature ad format native to conversation (like Google's text links or Facebook's News Feed ads)
