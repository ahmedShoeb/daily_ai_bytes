## Key Points

- Microsoft announces MAI-Cyber-1-Flash, a compact code-heavy security model integrated into MDASH (multi-agent vulnerability identification and remediation harness)
- The system delivers world-class performance at 50% cost reduction compared to leading models like GPT-5.4
- Designed to handle 90% of security tasks efficiently, reserving more expensive models for only the hardest 10% of cases
- Achieves 96% on CyberGym benchmark, beating competitors like Mythos, Gemini, and GPT by 12 points
- Part of a larger security ecosystem called Project Perception for continuous monitoring, patching, and threat mitigation
- Built on Microsoft's decades of security experience with trillions of daily signals across identity, endpoint, cloud, and network
- Features enterprise-grade controls including Role-Based Controls, tenant isolation, encryption, and sandboxed execution
- Developed with security-first calibration and rigorous testing by Microsoft's AI Red Team and third-party assessors
- Leverages Microsoft's unique reinforcement learning loop from 100+ trillion daily security signals and 1.6 million customers
- Represents Microsoft's first specialized cyber model built in-house from high-quality foundational data
