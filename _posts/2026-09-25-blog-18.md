## Key Points

- Derived data in AI training refers to creative content that is rewritten by AI before being used for training, which makes detection harder
- This practice allows AI companies to potentially bypass opt-outs by rewriting content rather than training directly on original works
- The problem is growing as AI models improve, making it increasingly difficult for creatives to know when their work is being exploited
- The only solution proposed is government-mandated training data transparency requirements for AI companies
- The UK government's new 'AI Skills Hub' has been criticized for including courses that don't exist, mislabeling paid courses as free, and containing biased content
- Some courses are described as thinly-veiled propaganda for AI companies, glossing over copyright controversies and IP issues
- The hub includes courses that present AI as 'miraculous' while ignoring lawsuits over training data and trivializing intellectual property concerns
