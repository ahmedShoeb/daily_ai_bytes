## Key Points

- NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform, co-designed across six products (Rubin GPU, Vera CPU, NVLink 6 Switch, etc.), shows up to 7x better token throughput per megawatt than previously claimed by Jensen Huang at GTC 2026
- Early pre-release testing shows Rubin NVL72 delivers approximately 67x higher total throughput per TCO compared to GB300 with TRTLLM, with performance improvements ranging from 1.4x to 3x in typical operating ranges (60-100 TPS)
- On agentic workloads, Rubin achieves 61% higher maximum P90 interactivity than GB300 (276.24 vs 171.53 TPS), with significantly better P90 end-to-end latency in high-throughput scenarios
- Performance comparisons show Rubin delivers 62% more tokens per rental TCO at 80 TPS compared to Blackwell Ultra, and at 60-100 million tokens per TCO dollar, Rubin's latency advantage ranges from 3x to 6x better than B200/B300
- Power efficiency analysis reveals Rubin delivers 59.4 million tok/s/MW at 100 TPS, representing 2.09x advantage over GB300's strongest engine and 29.5x advantage over AMD's MI355X
- Financial modeling indicates Rubin generates 39% more annual revenue and 42% more profit per gigawatt than GB300 at 75 TPS, translating to approximately $44.6 billion additional annual profit per GW
- The article highlights Jensen Huang's tendency to undersell performance claims ('sandbagging'), noting last time claimed 30x Hopper performance but achieved 98x when tested
- Benchmarking uses the industry-standard AgentX benchmark with validation from major cloud providers (Google, Microsoft, Azure), academic labs, and AI companies
