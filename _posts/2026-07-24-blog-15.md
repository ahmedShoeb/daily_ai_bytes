## Key Points

- Google introduces three new Gemini models: 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber
- Gemini 3.6 Flash offers 17% better token efficiency than 3.5 Flash with improved coding, knowledge work, and multimodal performance
- 3.6 Flash costs $1.50/1M input tokens and $7.50/1M output tokens - cheaper than 3.5 Flash
- Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is the fastest model in the 3.5 series at 350 output tokens per second with competitive pricing
- Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber is a specialized cybersecurity model available only to governments and trusted partners via CodeMender
- All models include enhanced Frontier Safety safeguards for CBRN and cyber offense misuses
- 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite are available today via Gemini API, Google AI Studio, and Gemini Enterprise
- Google has started pre-training for Gemini 4, its most ambitious model generation yet
