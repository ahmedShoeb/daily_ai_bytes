## Key Points

- StepFun's Step 5 Preview scores 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, matching Kimi K3 at ~2.8x lower cost per task, but lags in agentic evaluations
- Claude Opus 5.5 takes the top spot on the Intelligence Index with a score of 58, marking Anthropic's move to parity with GPT-6 Astra
- Anthropic cut Opus pricing 20% to $4/$20 per 1M input/output tokens and increased cache hit discounts
- Step 5 Preview is a 600B total parameter model with 27B active MoE parameters, priced at $1/$2.70 per 1M tokens
- Claude Opus 5.5 achieves leading scores on six of ten Intelligence Index evaluations and excels in agentic knowledge work
- Artificial Analysis announced Intelligence Index v4.2 with more private test sets and realistic tasks to prevent gaming
- New Endpoint Accuracy Index measures how much of an open weights model's accuracy each serverless API endpoint preserves
- SpaceXAI's Grok 4.5 scores 54 on the Intelligence Index, placing fourth overall and showing strong agentic performance
- Grok 4.5 is significantly cost-effective at $0.31 per Intelligence Index task and $2.59 per task on the Coding Agent Index
- Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview previously led the Intelligence Index, showing improvements in reasoning, knowledge, and hallucination reduction
