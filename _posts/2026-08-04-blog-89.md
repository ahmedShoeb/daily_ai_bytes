## Key Points

- Lilian Weng, co-founder of AI startup Thinking Machines, announced she was stepping down citing health issues from the stress and workload of startup life
- Weng stated in an internal Slack message and on X that she couldn't sustain the pace required at a startup due to physical health concerns
- Shortly after leaving Thinking Machines, OpenAI announced that Weng would be rejoining the company
- Weng previously worked at OpenAI as VP of AI Safety Research before co-founding Thinking Machines
- At OpenAI, Weng will lead a top-level team focused on accelerating internal research, particularly on recursive self-improvement (AI systems that can improve themselves)
- The move appears contradictory to her health-related departure reasons, though likely means less pressure than being a co-founder
- Thinking Machines co-founder Mira Murati (former OpenAI CTO) expressed support for Weng's health-focused decision, but it's unclear if she knew about the OpenAI move
- This high-profile talent move highlights the intense competition for AI talent among major labs
