## Key Points

- Google DeepMind has introduced two new AI models: Gemini 3.8 Live and Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking
- Gemini 3.8 Live is designed for scale and cost efficiency, combining conversational intelligence with fluid dialogue and visual grounding
- Gemini 3.8 Live Extended Thinking is built for high-complexity tasks with increased intelligence and multi-step reasoning
- The models rank #1 overall on Artificial Analysis' Speech to Speech Quality Index (82.6%) and lead in agentic task completion benchmarks
- The models can process visual inputs in near real-time and automatically detect and transition between 97 supported languages mid-conversation
- They can execute tools and API calls in the background while continuing conversations, and use early verbal cues for natural interaction
- Both models are rolling out starting today in various platforms including Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Search Live, and enterprise previews
- All audio generated by these AI products is watermarked with SynthID to help prevent misinformation
- The models are being integrated with developer platforms like Agora, Fishjam, LangChain, and partnerships with companies like Salesforce
- They will be available for everyone in Gemini Live and Google Workspace applications for subscribers
