## Key Points

Celeris introduces Celeris-1, a general purpose language model claiming near-GPT-5 level intelligence with 15x faster response times
The model uses a new diffusion-based inference architecture instead of conventional autoregressive generation
Celeris-1 achieves a p50 response latency of 157ms - 15× faster than GPT-5-mini and 17× faster than GPT-5
The model scores 76% on MMLU-Pro benchmark, compared to 78% for GPT-5-mini and 81% for GPT-5
Celeris-1 achieves 1,280 tokens per second throughput vs 144 for Gemini-3.5 Flash
The model is available starting today with sign up at celeris.ai
Celeris aims to maximize useful intelligence delivered per unit of time with this new architecture
Full benchmarks and technical details are available at celeris.ai/blog
