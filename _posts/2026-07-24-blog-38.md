## Key Points

- Gigatoken is a tokenization library that claims to be up to 1000x faster than HuggingFace's tokenizers
- It can tokenize text data at GB/s speeds and supports nearly all commonly used tokenizers
- The library offers both its own API and compatibility modes with HuggingFace Tokenizers and Tiktoken
- Performance benchmarks show dramatic improvements: GPT-2 tokenization at 24.53 GB/s vs HF's 24.8 MB/s (989x faster) on EPYC processors
- Apple M4 Max shows GPT-2 at 8.79 GB/s vs 6.9 MB/s (1,268x faster)
- Optimizations include SIMD implementation, caching pretoken mappings, and minimizing Python overhead
- The project claims you could tokenize the entirety of Common Crawl (130 trillion tokens) in just under 6.5 hours at EPYC speeds
- The author notes that AI was used in the final stages for API implementation, compatibility features, and profiling optimization
