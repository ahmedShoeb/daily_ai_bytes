## Key Points

- AIUC (Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company) launched by early Anthropic employee Rune Kvist and former METR COO Rajiv Dattani to address AI safety concerns
- The company has raised $55 million total, including a $40 million Series A led by Ribbit Capital, to create an audit and certification system for AI agents
- AIUC developed a standard called AIUC-1, modeled after cybersecurity standard SOC 2, with input from 250+ security and risk leaders
- The company runs AI agents through about 5,000 tests to evaluate performance on jailbreaks, hallucinations, and data leaks
- AIUC uses AI agents to conduct tests and AI to analyze results, but humans verify the final audits
- Customers include Cursor, Lovable, Harvey, and ElevenLabs, targeting enterprise adoption of AI systems
- The founders argue that as AI gets smarter, it becomes both harder to adopt and control in enterprise settings
- METR (Dattani's former employer) does similar testing for frontier AI labs, focusing on performance rather than safety auditing
- The approach aims to provide independent safety assessments to help enterprises make informed AI deployment decisions
