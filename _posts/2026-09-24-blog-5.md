## Key Points

- Meta's Muse AI personal agent has been downloaded over 2.5 million times since its September 8 launch, surpassing ChatGPT as the top free iOS app in the US
- The app is powered by Meta's Muse Spark AI models and offers monthly subscriptions at $20 or $100 levels for different usage tiers
- Muse represents Meta's major push into the AI agent market, allowing users to manage digital assistants that can perform tasks like filling forms and organizing emails
- The app's popularity boosted Meta's stock price by over 11% and led Wells Fargo to raise its price target to $796
- Amazon has blocked Muse from accessing its shopping site, citing privacy and security concerns about credential capture and data scraping
- Shopify is partnering with Meta to allow Muse access for agentic checkout in its online stores
- Muse surpassed rival AI apps like Anthropic's Claude and SpaceXAI's Grok during its first 13 days, with 1.5 million iOS downloads and 1.1 million Android downloads
- The launch comes amid broader societal concerns about AI safety and cybersecurity risks, and shortly after Meta's $17 billion settlement over alleged harms to children from its apps
- Bernstein analyst Stacy Rasgon noted this represents a shift toward broader consumer adoption of AI agents beyond just tech enthusiasts
