## Key Points

-bFidji Simo, former OpenAI executive and CEO of AGI deployment, left OpenAI to focus on curing chronic diseases like POTS after her own 7-year battle with the condition
bSimo founded ChronicleBio, a startup using AI to analyze biological data to find treatments for chronic conditions
bChronicleBio has collected 3,500 tubes of blood from 709 patients across 4 locations, extracting 153 terabytes of data—three times more than what GPT-3.5 was trained on
bThe company has identified 5 sub-diseases within POTS where the biology differs despite similar symptoms, enabling more targeted drug testing
bChronicleBio plans to launch mobile phlebotomy trucks starting August 11th, allowing home blood draws for chronic disease patients
bThe company will offer free in-depth reports for the first 250 participants, then charge $400 at cost to continue building their biological database
bChronic conditions are becoming an epidemic with hundreds of billions in lost productivity, and Simo believes AI is essential to solving these complex multisystem diseases
bSimo remains in contact with OpenAI leadership and continues advising while focusing on her recovery and ChronicleBio
bChronicleBio has raised $15 million and plans to test existing drugs on their patient population before year-end, then partner with biotech companies for new drug development
bSimo argues that AI tools combined with advanced biological data analysis can finally tackle diseases that were previously considered incurable
